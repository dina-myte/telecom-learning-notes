# What is NTN?

NTN stands for Non-Terrestrial Networks.

It refers to communication networks that operate above the Earth rather than only on the ground.

Examples:
- GEO satellites
- LEO constellations
- HAPS
- Direct-to-device satellites

## Why NTN matters

NTN can:
- extend mobile coverage
- support disaster recovery
- connect remote regions
- support maritime and aviation connectivity

## Key Trend

Telecom operators and satellite operators are increasingly integrating terrestrial and non-terrestrial connectivity into hybrid architectures.
